# Plan 07-01: Dual Ping-Pong Flash Persistence & Brownout Recovery

**Goal**: Implement dual ping-pong sector (A/B) atomic settings persistence with monotonic sequence numbering, CRC32 integrity verification, and brownout protection.

## Requirements
- `REQ-persistence-contract`: Dual sector allocation, atomic ping-pong commit, sequence arbitration, CRC32 integrity verification, and brownout fallback.
- `SETTINGS_VERSION`: Bump from `62u` to `63u` with `seq` header field.
- Zero data loss on power drops during erase or write.

## Success Criteria
1. `settings_t` header includes `uint32_t seq` after `version`; `SETTINGS_VERSION` bumped to 63u.
2. `settings_load()` scans Sector A and Sector B, validates magic, version, and CRC32, chooses newest valid sector via signed delta `(int32_t)(seq_a - seq_b) > 0`, or falls back to defaults if neither valid.
3. `settings_save()` targets inactive sector `1 - g_active_sector`, increments `g_seq` and `g_flash_erase_count`, halts motion, programs sector with interrupts disabled, and verifies readback before flipping `g_active_sector`.
4. `scripts/test_settings_parity.py` includes `seq` in `SKIP_FIELDS` and passes.
5. Host test executable `tests/host/test_persistence.c` validates all ping-pong, brownout corruption, erase-interruption, and wraparound behaviors.
6. `scripts/validate_regression.py` passes 100% (local build, sim build, unittest discover, ruff, diff hygiene).
