<objective>
Add the distance-based tension fault trip (Phase 13 Success Criterion 2): a second, lower threshold on the
accumulator FLARE already keeps, armed only after a real buffer transition, suppressed during deliberate
rail holds, reported in telemetry, and shipping on by default at 32 mm. Distance is the primary trip
because it scales with filament actually moved; the existing 6000 ms dwell timer becomes the slow-flow
fallback, and the emitted event says which one won.

Purpose: give a type-P rig a starvation trip that means the same thing at 5 mm/s and at 50 mm/s, and give
it one that still works on a rig whose rail reads shallow — where the pre-existing absolute-threshold
saturation guard never fires at all.
Output: `SYNC_TENSION_STOP_MM` as a live + flash-persisted knob, the trip and its arming/suppression rules
in `sync.c`, `TM:`/`ARM:` telemetry with a proven line budget, and sim coverage at two rail scales.
</objective>
