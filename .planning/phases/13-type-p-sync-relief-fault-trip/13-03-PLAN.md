<objective>
Resolve the type-P "+1.0 tension" ambiguity in firmware (Phase 13 Success Criterion 3): after one probe
distance worth of pinned relief feed — buffer travel, clamped to stay inside the distance-trip budget —
decide, from the best deflection observed across that whole window, whether the buffer is genuinely starved and being
refilled (a real consumer is pulling) or simply resting at its home rail with nothing moving, and act on
the answer immediately instead of burning the whole 32 mm / 6 s trip budget on a lane that is not going to
recover. The probe is a passive observation window over feed that is already happening — no extra motion,
no new counter, no absolute rail threshold.

Purpose: give the existing `(mode x filament_present)` resolution a third, firmware-local input so a
Klipper-agnostic controller can tell "home rail, no consumer" from "starved" without a host round-trip.
Output: the probe and its telemetry in firmware, a `PROBE:` bench command, sim coverage of both outcomes
at two rail scales, and the phase's software half closed with a green regression gate.
</objective>
