<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| TMC UART bus & stepper drivers | Hardware actuation boundary. Excessive current can cause stepper coil thermal overload or permanent driver damage. |
| Sync controller FSM state transitions | State machine integrity boundary. Boosted current must never persist into idle, pause, toolchange, or fault states. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-16-01 | Elevation of Privilege / Safety | `firmware/src/motion.c` (`tmc_apply_active_run_current`) | high | mitigate | Motor coil thermal damage or TMC2209 over-current trip from unconstrained boost current. Mitigated by hard hardware ceiling `clamp_i(current_ma, 0, 1200)` in `tmc_apply_active_run_current()` enforced on every write. |
| T-16-02 | Denial of Service | `firmware/src/sync.c` (`sync_check_tension_boost`) | medium | mitigate | UART bus flooding if current writes occur every control loop tick. Mitigated by strict edge detection (`!g_sync_tension_boost_active` on engage, `g_sync_tension_boost_active` on release); assertions in `test_tmc_boost.c` confirm zero redundant writes during sustained tension. |
| T-16-03 | Denial of Service / Safety | `firmware/src/sync.c` (`sync_set_state`, `sync_disable`, `stop_all`) | high | mitigate | Motor left in boosted 1200 mA state after pause, stop, or fault, causing motor overheating while stationary. Mitigated by centralized unwind in `sync_set_state()` for all non-`SYNC_ACTIVE` transitions, paired with defense-in-depth unwind calls in `stop_all()` and `sync_disable()`. |
| T-16-04 | Tampering / State Desync | `firmware/src/settings_store.c` (`sync_tmc_settings`) | medium | mitigate | TMC heartbeat brownout recovery clobbering active boost or detecting false register mismatch during idle probe. Mitigated by updating `g_shadow_ihold_irun` and `g_shadow_vsense` atomically with boost writes and querying active current via `sync_get_active_run_current_ma()` during recovery. |
</threat_model>
